# CHSH Simulation

This simulation aims to teach users about the limitations of local hidden-variable theories compared to non-local quantum mechanics. It provides a virtual CHSH game sandbox where users can test and play with different strategies — both classical and quantum.

## How to Play

The games comprises of two modes, Classical and Quantum. In each game mode, users construct their own strategies to maximize their success rate, actively engaging with the theoretical limits of each approach.

* **Classical Mode:** Users utilize probabilistic and deterministic methods, ultimately discovering that the best possible classical strategy is capped at a 75% success rate.

* **Quantum Mode:** Users incorporate spin measurements alongside their classical logic. The simulation displays a side-by-side comparison of a non-local entangled particle pair versus a pair whose spins were predetermined (a local hidden-variable model).

This direct comparison allows users to see exactly how non-local strategies can break the classical 75% limit and reach the quantum maximum of ~85%. By watching the game play out based on their custom strategies, users gain a much firmer, intuitive grasp of the fundamental differences between local and non-local mechanics.
